# Sky Stone Dust

<ItemImage id="sky_dust" scale="4" />

A <ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" /> block that has been crushed by an <ItemLink id="inscriber" />. Used in the production of
<ItemLink id="cell_component_256k" /> and <ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" />.

## Where to find?

<ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" /> Can be traded from [Fluix Researcher](ae2:items-blocks-machines/fluix_researcher.md) or Wandering Trader.

Telepad Structures for each ring will contain a few <ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" />. 

The <Color color="#FFFF55">Void Ring</Color> will generate <ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" /> in the lower sections.
